## Why

Friends splitting a shared expense need a quick way to see an equal share. The 平帳 landing page introduces the tool and lets visitors try it immediately.

## What Changes

- Add a Traditional Chinese landing page with an equal-share calculator.
- Accept a total in New Taiwan dollars and a positive whole-number participant count, then display the share on calculation.
- Show an error for invalid entries and provide a button that clears the calculator.

### New Capabilities

- `equal-split-calculator`: Landing page, equal-share calculation, validation, and reset.
